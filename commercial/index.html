Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods pull conditioned air out nonstop, and the makeup air unit has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. Tallahassee HVAC services, sizes, and repairs the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep both the kitchen and the dining room comfortable, and we schedule service around your operating hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical concerns for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with documented service records that show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. We provide detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

Replacement and maintenance plans are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. When a job can't happen during service, we do it after close, keeping your kitchen cooking and guests comfortable throughout.

Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm proper venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

A commercial HVAC failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens get too hot to work, shoppers walk out, server rooms overheat, and tenants start complaining. Our commercial line gets a technician rolling fast, throughout Tallahassee and the surrounding area.
